Peacely Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Peacely is a property, PG, tenant and rent management application. This Privacy Policy explains, at a practical level, what information may be handled when an owner uses Peacely.

1. Information you provide

Depending on how you use Peacely, information may include the owner's name, email address and phone number; property, room and bed information; tenant names, phone numbers and optional email addresses; rent, deposit, invoice and payment records; move-in and move-out information; and optional tenant identity-proof details or document images entered by the owner.

2. Payment information

Peacely's current rent-payment model is direct payment to the property owner. An owner may store a UPI ID, phone number, QR code and payment instructions. Peacely does not take a percentage of a tenant's rent through this owner-direct flow. The owner confirms a payment in Peacely after receiving it.

3. WhatsApp

Peacely can prepare a WhatsApp message containing a payment or receipt link. The owner chooses to open WhatsApp and send the message. Peacely does not use a WhatsApp Business API to automatically send these messages in the current owner workflow.

4. How information is used

Information is used to provide property management, tenant records, room and bed allocation, invoices, payment records, reminders, reports and related account features requested by the owner.

5. Data access and account separation

Owner accounts are separated using authenticated sessions and server-side ownership checks. Owners should keep their login credentials private and should only enter tenant information they are authorised to manage.

6. Tenant documents

If an owner uploads identity-proof images or other tenant information, the owner is responsible for having an appropriate lawful basis and informing the tenant as required by applicable law. Owners should avoid uploading information that is unnecessary for their legitimate property-management purpose.

7. Service providers

Peacely may rely on infrastructure or third-party services for hosting, database operation, messaging links and other technical functions. Those services may process information as necessary to provide the service and may have their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data deletion

An owner can use the account deletion feature in Peacely. Account deletion is intended to permanently delete the owner's Peacely account and associated application records, subject to technical, legal or security requirements that may require limited retention.

9. Security

Peacely uses authenticated sessions and server-side access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so owners should use a strong password, protect their devices and avoid sharing account credentials.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when Peacely's features, operating practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be made available through Peacely.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact chillinpeace.team@gmail.com. Current support hours are Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–6 PM.

This page describes Peacely's current operating model and is not legal advice.
