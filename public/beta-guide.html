Peacely Beta Owner Guide

For the first 5–10 PG/property owners

Start here

  1. Create your Peacely owner account.
  2. Add your property.
  3. Add floors if you use them.
  4. Add rooms inside the property.
  5. Add beds inside each room.
  6. Add tenants and assign each tenant to an available room and bed.

Monthly rent workflow

  1. Check Invoices for the tenant's current rent.
  2. Open the invoice/payment action when the tenant pays.
  3. For owner-direct UPI payments, the tenant pays the property owner directly.
  4. After you receive the money, use Mark as Paid in Peacely.
  5. Use the WhatsApp action when you want to send the invoice, payment link or receipt message.

When a tenant changes room

Open the tenant details and use Change Room / Bed. Select the property, room and available bed. Peacely updates the assignment and bed occupancy.

When a tenant moves out

Start the move-out notice from the tenant record. The current workflow uses a one-month notice period. When the move-out date is reached, the tenant is made inactive and the assigned bed is released by the payment automation process.

What to test during beta

  • Sign up and log in on your phone.
  • Create one property, several rooms and several beds.
  • Add and move tenants between beds.
  • Record a real or test payment and verify the invoice status.
  • Open the WhatsApp message and verify the wording before sending.
  • Add the owner's UPI ID/QR and check the public payment page.
  • Start a move-out notice and verify the displayed date.
  • Export your available data backup from the app.

Report problems

Please send the exact screen, what you tapped, what you expected and what happened. Screenshots are especially useful. Contact chillinpeace.team@gmail.com, Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–6 PM.

Beta note

Peacely is being tested with a small group of real owners. Features may change as feedback is incorporated. Do not rely on Peacely as your only record until your beta testing is complete; keep your normal business records available during this period.
